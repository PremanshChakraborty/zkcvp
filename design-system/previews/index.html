Three rules this system encodes

A negative verdict is not a system error
The column is named eval_failed, but it means the Evaluator returned not satisfied — not that anything broke. It renders as "Not satisfied" in periwinkle. Rate limits, crashes and timeouts are a separate component in coral, and the two never share a hue.
Sealed is not unverifiable
An evidence bundle's contents are withheld while its digest stays checkable. Verifying integrity and disclosing content are separate operations, and the evidence component keeps the verify action live to make that visible.
Never fake a percentage
Evaluation blocks the developer's own request and has no honest progress fraction, so the progress bar is indeterminate and the elapsed clock warns as it nears the platform's execution ceiling.
