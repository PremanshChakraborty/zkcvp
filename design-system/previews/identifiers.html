Identifiers

SHAs, digests, repo names, log references. These are the product's primary nouns, so they share one visual language: monospace, inset, selectable, copyable.

Commit SHA

Short form on screen; the full forty characters stay in the tooltip and the copy payload. Claims pin exact commits, so the full value must always be recoverable from the UI without a round trip. Hover to reveal copy.

Hash digest

Truncates in the middle, never at the end. When someone eyeballs a digest against another source they compare the head and the tail — a trailing ellipsis would destroy the only affordance that matters. This is the evidence_hash that makes an evidence bundle checkable without disclosing any of its contents.
