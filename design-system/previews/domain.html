Verdict report

One card per entry in report.perRequirement. Rationales are styled as prose, deliberately not as code blocks. The Evaluator is constrained at generation time never to emit verbatim source, and a leak would render as visibly wrong here. That is a weak free signal, not a filter, and must not be relied on as one.

Sealed evidence

Two facts have to land at once, and they are easy to conflate. The bundle's contents are not viewable, because it holds real source from a private repo and no disclosure mechanism exists yet. But its digest is still independently checkable. Verifying that evidence was never altered and disclosing what it says are separate operations, and the first never requires the second, which is why the verify action stays live while the content stays sealed.

Evidence bundle sealed · sha256:9f2a41d0…f3c40b Contents are not disclosed in this phase. The digest can still be checked against the log without revealing them.

Audit timeline

A superseded version's record is history, not an error. A result that lands after an edit writes to the version it was invoked against and stays there as accurate history, so old entries are never dimmed into looking like mistakes.
